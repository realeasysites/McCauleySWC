About

Heart, grit and creativity in every detail

McCauley Stoneworks & Craft is a Portland-based stonework and hardscape company serving York and Cumberland Counties. With 15 years of experience, the focus is simple: take your ideas and turn them into something solid, beautiful and built to last. Stone fights back, so it takes patience, balance and a few good counterpunches.

From elegant walkways and patios to durable stone and block walls, granite steps, fire pits and tile, each job is approached as a craft, not a quick install. We go the full rounds on every one.
